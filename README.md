# Kepani Digital | Social Media & Digital Marketing Strategy

This repository documents Kepani Digital’s public-facing social media management and digital marketing strategy.

## Project Overview

Kepani Digital uses a coordinated digital ecosystem to communicate its work across AI, technology, business development, education, and responsible innovation.

This project documents how the company organizes its social media presence, digital marketing activity, platform roles, and public-facing communications.

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to establish a clear, repeatable, and responsible approach to managing Kepani Digital’s digital presence.

The strategy emphasizes consistency, audience relevance, platform alignment, responsible AI communications, and long-term brand stewardship.

## Strategic Focus

* Maintain consistent messaging across digital platforms
* Align content with the purpose and audience of each platform
* Support Kepani Digital projects, research, and applied AI initiatives
* Use AI as a decision-support and content-development tool while retaining human judgment
* Protect trust by avoiding misleading, exaggerated, or unsupported claims
* Maintain a sustainable publishing workflow rather than maximizing posting volume

## Platform Alignment

Kepani Digital uses different platforms for different communication purposes, including professional networking, project documentation, public education, visual content, and community engagement.

GitHub and Notion provide structured project documentation, while public social platforms support communication, discovery, and engagement.

## Responsible AI Communications

AI-assisted content should remain subject to human review.

Public communications should clearly reflect Kepani Digital’s values of responsible AI adoption, ethical decision-making, transparency, accountability, and respect for the communities affected by technology.
